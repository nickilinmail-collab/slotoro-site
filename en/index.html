Is Online Gambling Legal in Iceland?

Iceland does not have a domestic licensing system for online casinos. The state-run lottery (Happdrætti Íslands) is the only regulated gambling option. However, there is no specific law that prohibits individual Icelanders from playing at internationally licensed online casinos.

Most casinos serving Icelandic players operate under licenses from Malta (MGA), Curaçao, or the UK Gambling Commission. We only review casinos with valid international licenses.
