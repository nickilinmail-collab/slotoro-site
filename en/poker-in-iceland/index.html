Is Poker Legal in Iceland?

Poker falls under Iceland's general gambling laws. Private, organized poker games for money are technically illegal under Icelandic law. Home games among friends in a small, social setting exist in a grey area, but commercial poker rooms and tournaments are not permitted.

Online poker at internationally licensed sites is not specifically prohibited for individuals, similar to online casino play.

History of Poker in Iceland

Iceland has produced several notable poker players who compete internationally. The small population (about 380,000) has a disproportionate number of skilled players, partly due to the country's strong tradition of card games and strategic thinking.

Icelanders typically play poker online or travel to live poker events in other European countries, particularly the UK, Malta, and Spain.
